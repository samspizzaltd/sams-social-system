Privacy Policy

Sam's Social Studio — effective 14 November 2026 (replaces the version of 11 November 2026)

Sam's Social Studio ("the Service") is a social media management application operated by Sam's Pizza Ltd (შპს „სემის პიცა“, company ID 406255127, 100 Mirian Mepe St, Tbilisi, Georgia). It is an internal business tool: it prepares and publishes the business's own videos to the social media accounts owned by Sam's Pizza Ltd (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube). This policy explains what data the Service handles.

1. YouTube API Services

The Service uses YouTube API Services to upload the business's own videos to the business's own YouTube channel, after the channel owner has approved each video. By connecting a YouTube channel to the Service, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The channel owner can revoke the Service's access to the YouTube channel at any time from the Google Account security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, or with the "Disconnect" button in the Service's control panel.

2. Data the Service accesses

When the business owner connects one of the business's accounts through the platform's official authorization flow, the Service receives and stores:

  • YouTube (through YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's Google OAuth consent): the connected channel's ID and title, used only to show which channel videos go to; and the OAuth access and refresh tokens Google issues for the permissions "upload videos" (youtube.upload) and "view your YouTube account" (youtube.readonly). The Service also records the ID and link of each video it uploaded and, every 3 hours, reads the public statistics of those videos (view, like and comment counts) and the channel's subscriber count, to show the business owner in the control panel how the business's own videos perform. It does not read the channel's other videos, the text of comments, who the subscribers are, YouTube Analytics reports, or any data about viewers.
  • TikTok, Instagram, Facebook: the connected account's basic profile information (account ID, display name, avatar), the access token the platform issues, and metadata about the business's own posts (IDs, captions, view counts and similar statistics) used for scheduling and reporting.
  • Instagram trending sounds (through Meta's Instagram Audio API): Instagram's list of currently trending music and sounds - each sound's ID, title, artist or the creator's public username, length, cover image and a temporary preview link - read every few hours so the business owner can choose sounds for the business's own Instagram posts. It does not read who uses those sounds or anything about them.

3. Data the Service does NOT collect

The Service has no public user base and no public sign-up. It does not collect personal data from platform users, followers, commenters, viewers or website visitors. It does not track, profile or advertise to individuals, it does not sell data, and it does not let third parties serve content or advertising, or place or read cookies, in its pages. The control panel keeps its own login session in the browser's local storage only.

4. How data is used

Stored tokens and account data are used only to upload and publish the business's own content to the business's own accounts, to show which account a post goes to, to confirm that an upload finished, to read the performance statistics of the business's own posts, and to suggest trending Instagram sounds the owner may approve for the business's own Instagram posts. Every post is approved by the business owner before it is published.

5. Sharing

Data is not shared with, or sold to, anyone. To do its work the Service sends data only to:

  • the platforms' official APIs — YouTube API Services (Google), TikTok and Meta — to perform the actions described above;
  • Anthropic (the Claude API), which receives a few still frames and the title of the business's own videos to draft captions. No YouTube API data, no tokens and no personal data are sent to Anthropic.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored on the Service's own server infrastructure in access-controlled databases. Tokens are never displayed in the interface and are reachable only by the Service's backend. Access to the Service requires authentication.

7. Retention and deletion

  • YouTube: "Disconnect" in the control panel revokes the Service's access at Google and deletes the stored tokens, channel data and video statistics immediately. If access is revoked from the Google Account instead, the Service notices it within a day, deletes the tokens at once and the remaining channel data and statistics within 7 days. Video statistics and the subscriber count are refreshed every 3 hours; any that could not be refreshed for 30 days are deleted.
  • Other platforms: account data and tokens are kept only while the account stays connected. Disconnecting an account in the control panel, or revoking the app's access in the platform's own settings, invalidates the stored token.
  • Any stored data is deleted on request to the contact below.
