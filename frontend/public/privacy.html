Privacy Policy

Sam's Social Studio — effective 13 October 2026

Sam's Social Studio ("the Service") is a social media management application operated by Sam's Pizza Ltd (შპს „სემის პიცა“, company ID 406255127, 100 Mirian Mepe St, Tbilisi, Georgia). It manages the social media accounts owned by Sam's Pizza Ltd. This policy explains what data the Service handles.

1. Data the Service accesses

When the business owner connects a social media account (for example a TikTok, Instagram or Facebook account) through the platform's official authorization flow, the Service receives and stores:

  • the connected account's basic profile information (account ID, display name, avatar);
  • an access token issued by the platform, used solely to act on that account;
  • metadata about the business's own posts (IDs, captions, view counts and similar statistics) used for scheduling and performance reporting.

2. Data the Service does NOT collect

The Service has no public user base. It does not collect personal data from platform users, followers, commenters or website visitors. It does not track, profile or advertise to individuals, and it does not sell or share data with third parties.

3. How data is used

Stored tokens and account data are used only to upload and publish the business's own marketing content to the business's own accounts, and to read the performance statistics of that content. Every post is approved by the business owner before publishing.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on the Service's own server infrastructure in access-controlled databases. Access tokens are never displayed in the interface and are reachable only by the Service's backend. Access to the Service requires authentication.

5. Retention and deletion

Account data and tokens are kept only while the account stays connected. Disconnecting an account in the control panel, or revoking the app's access in the platform's own settings, invalidates the stored token; stored account data is deleted on request to the contact below.

6. Third parties

The Service communicates with the connected platforms' official APIs (TikTok, Meta) to perform the actions described above, and with no other third party.

7. Contact
